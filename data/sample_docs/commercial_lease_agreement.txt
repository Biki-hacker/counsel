COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

Landlord: Metro Commercial Properties LLC
Tenant: Innovation Labs Private Limited
Premises: Suite 400, 100 Tech Park Way
Term: 36 Months commencing March 1, 2025

1. RENT AND SECURITY DEPOSIT
Monthly base rent is $4,500. Security deposit is $13,500. Rent increases 5% annually on the anniversary date.

2. AUTOMATIC ROLLOVER / RENEWAL
This lease automatically renews for an additional twenty-four (24) months unless Tenant delivers formal certified notice of non-renewal exactly one hundred and twenty (120) days prior to expiration.

3. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR
Tenant bears full financial responsibility for all HVAC systems, electrical installations, plumbing fixtures, and routine structural repairs exceeding $250.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT FORFEITURE AND ACCELERATED RENT
If Tenant vacates prior to lease termination, Landlord retains the complete $13,500 deposit as liquidated damages and accelerates all unpaid rent for the remainder of the 36-month term immediately.

5. RESTORATION TO SHELL CONDITION
Upon termination, Tenant must at its sole expense remove all custom partitions, network cabling, and improvements, restoring the premises to bare concrete shell condition.
